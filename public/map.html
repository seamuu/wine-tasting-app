Wine Flight · map round

Where in Italy?

One A4 map, and a small square of tracing paper each. Slide your square over the spot you think the wine came from, mark one dot, and leave it there while it’s photographed. The four black corner squares stay uncovered on purpose — they are what tells the app where your dot landed. Who you are and which wine it is get picked in the app as it scans, so the same map is used all night.
