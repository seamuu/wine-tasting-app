Filled-in tasting cards

Only tasting cards are scanned. Team quiz and map answers are entered by the host. These are synthetic practice answers, not facts about your bottles.

For the simplest test, open Try a demo first on the home page and choose Create demo tasting. The defaults here match it: colours + animals, two wines, no individual price row, A white and B red.

Each page contains one card. On reMarkable, open a single-card PDF full screen. For paper, print single-sided at actual size. You can also upload an individual PNG directly to the scanner.

For the default demo tasting setup: Download PDF · one card per page · Download four PNGs. These downloads use two wines, colours + animals, and no price row. For other settings, update the cards above and use Print.
