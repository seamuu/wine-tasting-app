# Quick test

1. On the laptop, open http://localhost:3000. Use your existing fictional tasting, or open **Try a demo first** and click **Create demo tasting →**. A new demo has two fictional wines, two guests and sample team quiz answers. Creating another demo starts fresh; it does not reset an existing tasting.
2. Display [A-1.pdf](http://localhost:3000/practice-cards/A-1.pdf) full-screen on reMarkable. Each PDF contains exactly one card. Other cards: [A-2](http://localhost:3000/practice-cards/A-2.pdf), [B-1](http://localhost:3000/practice-cards/B-1.pdf), [B-2](http://localhost:3000/practice-cards/B-2.pdf). You can also download [four PNGs](http://localhost:3000/practice-cards/practice-card-images.zip) for a laptop upload test.
3. Leave the laptop on **Collect cards** and scan wine A’s QR code with your phone. Use the same Wi-Fi. Tap **Scan a card** to open the camera. Photograph one card with all four black corners visible.
4. The phone reads it automatically. Check the one original photo and the summary beneath it, then choose **Confirm & save** or **Retake**. Expect Red Fox, wine A, rating 8 for A-1.
5. Tap **Scan next card**, then photograph A-2. Expect Red Owl, rating 6. After confirming, the phone shows **All cards scanned — 2/2 saved. You can close this screen now.** The laptop updates to **All cards collected** automatically.
6. On the laptop, continue to **Team answers**. Demo answers are already filled in; save and reveal. Continue through the bottle, tasting results, quiz results and standings. Both sample wines average **7/10** when both cards are saved.
7. Continue to wine B, scan its new QR code, then repeat with B-1 and B-2. Each QR is pinned to its wine; opening A’s QR never silently switches to B.

Only individual tasting cards are scanned. The host enters real teams’ quiz and map answers manually. Re-scanning and confirming the same person + wine replaces that card, so it does not increase the collected count.

For entry without a photo, choose **Enter card manually** at the top of the phone
scanner or in the laptop’s collection panel. Fill any rating, structure and
flavours from the card, then **Save card**. Names are optional. Each save opens a
fresh form for the same wine; the laptop count updates as usual.

Setup is one vertical flow: **The tasting → Teams → Wines → Print**. Save the first section to continue to Teams. Card settings are optional, and **Start the quiz** stays visible at the top. Wine details, score breakdowns and other secondary controls are collapsed until needed. The optional guest TV view is under **More options → Show on a TV**.

For cards matching a different tasting, use **Filled-in cards for a scan test ↗** under Setup → Print. Those settings must match the tasting’s names, wine count and price row. The downloadable PDFs above match the default two-wine demo.
